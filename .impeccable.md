### Brand Personality
Bold, playful, expressive. Christopher loves purple, saturated colour, simple oversized typography, considered imagery, motion, cultural references, and abstract art. He wants visitors to feel seen and each interaction to feel like an experience.

### Aesthetic Direction
An image-led, kinetic editorial portfolio, inspired by the craft of Awwwards sites. The accepted direction is a rich purple opening, responsive collage, distinctive project reveals, and a living gallery. The Design Poet connects typography (voice), imagery (metaphor), and motion (rhythm). Preserve light and dark themes and existing CMS content.

### Design Principles
1. Make the identity recognizable through colour, scale, composition, and language.
2. Give imagery an active role in the layout; preserve honest project content.
3. Make motion expressive and coherent, with deliberate pauses for reading.
4. Design for touch, keyboard, smaller screens, and reduced motion.
5. Current work includes local polish, case-study presentation, and admin editing/sync reliability. Preserve authored content and verify failed saves and recovery. Server-verified authentication remains separate from these UI and persistence changes.
